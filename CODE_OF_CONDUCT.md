# Neo N4 Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Neo N4 community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### ✅ Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Demonstrating empathy and kindness** toward other people
- **Being respectful** of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- **Giving and gracefully accepting** constructive feedback
- **Accepting responsibility** and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- **Focusing on what is best** not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
- **Being patient** with newcomers and those learning
- **Recognizing and celebrating** the contributions of others
- **Assuming good intentions** unless proven otherwise

### ❌ Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment of any kind**, including unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- **Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments**, and personal or political attacks
- **Public or private harassment**, including sustained disruption of discussions
- **Publishing others' private information**, such as physical or email addresses, without explicit permission ("doxxing")
- **Violent threats or language** directed against another person
- **Sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, or otherwise discriminatory** jokes and language
- **Posting or displaying sexually explicit or violent material**
- **Advocating for, or encouraging**, any of the above behavior
- **Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate** in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Neo N4 community spaces, including but not limited to:

- **GitHub repositories** (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews)
- **Official communication channels** (Discord, Telegram, Slack, mailing lists)
- **Community events** (meetups, conferences, hackathons, both online and offline)
- **Social media** (when representing the Neo N4 project)
- **Direct messages** between community members about Neo N4 matters

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

## Community Guidelines

### Contributing to Neo N4

We welcome contributions from everyone! To ensure a smooth collaboration:

#### Code Contributions
- **Follow the [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) guidelines**
- **Write clear commit messages** describing what and why
- **Include tests** for new features and bug fixes
- **Document your code** with comments where necessary
- **Be open to feedback** during code review
- **Respect maintainers' time** - they are often volunteers

#### Reporting Bugs
- **Search existing issues** before creating a new one
- **Provide clear reproduction steps** with minimal examples
- **Include environment details** (OS, version, configuration)
- **Be patient** - maintainers will respond when available
- **Don't demand immediate fixes** - this is open source

#### Asking Questions
- **Use appropriate channels** (GitHub Discussions for design questions, Discord for general help)
- **Search first** - your question may already be answered
- **Provide context** - what are you trying to achieve?
- **Be specific** - vague questions get vague answers
- **Thank those who help** - show appreciation

### Communication Best Practices

#### In Discussions
- **Stay on topic** - create new threads for new topics
- **Be concise** - respect others' time
- **Quote appropriately** - provide context without excessive quoting
- **Use inclusive language** - avoid assumptions about technical knowledge or background
- **Disagree respectfully** - focus on ideas, not people

#### In Code Reviews
- **Be constructive** - suggest improvements, don't just criticize
- **Explain your reasoning** - help others learn
- **Accept that not all suggestions will be adopted** - maintainers make final decisions
- **Don't take feedback personally** - it's about the code, not you
- **Acknowledge good work** - praise is motivating

#### Across Time Zones
- **Be patient with response times** - the community is global
- **Use asynchronous communication** - don't expect instant replies
- **Document decisions** - not everyone can attend synchronous meetings
- **Record meetings** when possible and share notes

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

### Current Community Leaders

- **Security Council** - security@neo-n4.network
- **Core Maintainers** - Listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)
- **Community Moderators** - Listed in Discord/Telegram channel descriptions

## Reporting Violations

### When to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it as soon as possible.

### How to Report

**For public violations** (e.g., in GitHub issues, Discord):
- **Flag the content** using platform tools (report button, flag emoji)
- **Notify a moderator** in the channel
- **Send a direct message** to a community leader

**For private or serious violations:**
- **Email:** conduct@neo-n4.network
- **Anonymous form:** https://neo-n4.network/report (if available)
- **Direct message** a community leader you trust

### What to Include in Your Report

- **Your contact information** (so we can follow up)
- **Names (usernames) of people involved** (including witnesses)
- **Description of the incident** (what happened, when, where)
- **Any supporting evidence** (screenshots, links, logs)
- **Whether you've already taken any action** (e.g., asked them to stop)
- **Whether you want the report to be confidential**

### What Happens After You Report

1. **Acknowledgment** - Within 24 hours, we'll confirm receipt
2. **Investigation** - We'll review the report and gather additional information if needed
3. **Decision** - Community leaders will decide on appropriate action
4. **Communication** - We'll inform you of the outcome (respecting privacy of all involved)
5. **Appeal** - If you disagree with the decision, you can appeal within 7 days

**Confidentiality:** All reports will be handled with discretion. We will not share reporter identity without explicit permission, except when required by law.

## Enforcement

### Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines in determining consequences for violations:

#### 1. Correction (Minor Violation)

**Community Impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

**Example:** First-time use of mildly inappropriate language in a heated technical discussion.

#### 2. Warning (Moderate Violation)

**Community Impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time (typically 7-30 days). This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

**Example:** Repeatedly dismissing others' technical concerns in a disrespectful manner after being asked to stop.

#### 3. Temporary Ban (Serious Violation)

**Community Impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time (typically 30-90 days). No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

**Example:** Harassing another community member, making discriminatory comments, or doxxing.

#### 4. Permanent Ban (Severe or Repeated Violations)

**Community Impact:** Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

**Example:** Repeated harassment, threats of violence, or sustained toxicity after previous warnings.

### Special Cases

**Security-related violations** (e.g., threatening to exploit vulnerabilities if demands aren't met) may result in immediate permanent ban and reporting to law enforcement.

**Spam or commercial solicitation** will result in immediate content removal and temporary ban (permanent if repeated).

**Impersonation** of community members or team members will result in immediate permanent ban.

## Appeals Process

If you believe an enforcement action was unjust:

1. **Submit an appeal** to conduct-appeals@neo-n4.network within 7 days
2. **Include:**
   - Original incident description
   - Enforcement action taken
   - Why you believe it was unjust
   - Any new evidence
3. **Review by different community leaders** (not involved in original decision)
4. **Decision within 14 days** - Final decision is binding

Appeals are granted in cases of:
- Mistaken identity
- Procedural errors
- New evidence significantly changes context
- Punishment disproportionate to violation

## Scope of Enforcement

### What We Can Enforce

- Behavior in official Neo N4 spaces (GitHub, Discord, Telegram, events)
- Use of Neo N4 branding in unethical ways
- Impersonation of Neo N4 team members
- Security-related threats against the project

### What We Cannot Enforce

- Behavior in private spaces unrelated to Neo N4
- Personal disputes that don't involve Neo N4 activities
- Disagreements about technical decisions (those are resolved through governance)
- Legal matters (those should be handled through legal channels)
